How It Works

Three things. That’s the whole app.

You never open the app before driving. You never press start. $MILES detects when you roll and does the rest.

STEP 01 🏎️

Just drive

Pull out of your driveway and go. $MILES wakes up when you cross 20 mph on public roads and stops when you park.

STEP 02 ⚡

Queue TSLA crumbs

Each qualified mile earns tokenized TSLA on Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663). Payouts accumulate straight to your wallet.

Climb the ranks

Frequently asked questions

$MILES leverages Apple’s low-power CoreLocation significant change API and CoreMotion activity recognition. Full GPS only fires once vehicular motion is confirmed at 20+ mph, conserving battery while keeping tracking precise.
Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) is a specialized layer-2 blockchain for tokenized real-world assets. Your verified miles queue fractional tokenized TSLA rewards directly to your on-chain address. Not affiliated with Tesla Inc.
The 20 mph threshold ensures only genuine vehicular travel on mapped roads is rewarded — joggers, cyclists, and stationary phone shaking are strictly excluded by physics scoring.
