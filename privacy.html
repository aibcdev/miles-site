Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 16, 2026

Short version: MILES uses your location and motion data to detect driving trips automatically. We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. Location data stays on your device except for the trip distance figures we compute and store for your own history.

1. Who We Are

MILES ("the app", "we", "us") is an independent drive-tracking application. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Tesla, Inc., Robinhood Markets, Inc., or any of their subsidiaries.

2. What Data We Collect

2a. Location Data

MILES requests "Always Allow" location permission so it can detect driving trips automatically in the background — even when the app is not open.

  • We collect GPS coordinates during a detected trip to measure distance and verify road usage.
  • Trip route coordinates are stored on your device only (via SwiftData).
  • We do not upload or transmit raw GPS coordinates to any server.
  • We do not build a live location map visible to other users.
  • We do not sell location data to any third party.

2b. Motion & Fitness Data

We use CMMotionActivityManager to distinguish car driving from walking or cycling. This prevents false trip detections from pedestrians carrying a phone at speed. Motion data is processed locally and is never transmitted or stored beyond the current session.

2c. Trip Statistics

Computed trip statistics (distance, duration, top speed, timestamp) are stored locally on your device. Aggregated totals (total miles, paid miles) may be submitted to our leaderboard API in anonymized form, associated only with your Robinhood Chain wallet address — not your name, email, or phone number.

2d. Wallet Address

You may optionally provide a Robinhood Chain wallet address (a public blockchain address). This is stored locally and submitted to our API only to associate your paid miles with your wallet for token distribution. Wallet addresses are public by nature of blockchain technology.

2e. Notifications

If you grant notification permission, we send local push notifications when a trip ends. We do not use push tokens for marketing.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Photos, contacts, or microphone access
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • Any data linked to your Apple ID
  • Payment information

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Trip detection — to automatically start and stop recording a drive
  • Distance measurement — to calculate verified paid miles
  • Road verification — to confirm driving occurred on a mapped public road
  • Leaderboard — to display anonymized mileage rankings (wallet address only)
  • Token distribution — to queue TSLA crumb payouts to your wallet address

5. Third-Party Services

  • Apple MapKit — used for the map display. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) — a public blockchain. Transactions on-chain are public by design.

We do not integrate any advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase, Mixpanel), or social login providers.

6. Data Retention

  • Trip data on your device: retained until you delete the app or clear app data.
  • Leaderboard entries (wallet address + mileage): retained for the duration of the program.
  • You can delete all local data by deleting the app from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

MILES is rated 4+ on the App Store but is intended for licensed drivers (typically 16+). We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

Because we don't collect personally identifiable information, most data deletion is accomplished simply by deleting the app. For any leaderboard data associated with your wallet address, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have rights under GDPR including access, rectification, and erasure. Contact us to exercise these rights.

9. Security

All communication with our API uses HTTPS/TLS. Trip data is stored in SwiftData on your device, which is protected by iOS device encryption. We do not store raw GPS data on our servers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted in the App Store "What's New" section and by updating the effective date above.
